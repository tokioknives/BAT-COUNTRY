# 🦇 Bat Country: A Neon Desert Fever Dream

A top-down, open-world, 7-chapter browser game. No install, no dependencies, just open and play.

**▶ Play it:** https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/bat-country/

## Run locally
Double-click `index.html`, or serve the folder:

    python3 -m http.server 8000

then visit http://localhost:8000

## Controls
**Desktop:** click the game once to focus it.

- **WASD / Arrow keys**: move
- **E**: get in/out of the car, interact
- **Space**: take a capsule

## Phone / tablet
On touch devices an on-screen joystick (left) and buttons (right) appear automatically:
- **Joystick**: move
- **E**: get in/out of the car
- **CAP**: take a capsule
- **START** (or tap the screen): advance menus

Best played in landscape. Jump to a chapter with `?level=3` in the URL.
